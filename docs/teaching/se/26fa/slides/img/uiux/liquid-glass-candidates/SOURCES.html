Liquid Glass launch-era screenshot candidates

Selected assets now appear in the lecture: notifications-beto.jpg, control-holly.jpg, verge-beta1.png, and verge-beta2.png. The other assets remain research candidates. These concern the original iOS 26 developer beta in June 2025, not iOS 27. Image pixels remain unchanged. The two Verge downloads returned JPEG data despite their .png URLs; they were losslessly re-encoded as PNG for Typst compatibility. Complaints are individual reactions, not measured usability findings.
