Technology background

  • Analog Devices: https://www.analog.com/en/resources/analog-dialogue/articles/the-pda-challenge-met.html
  • Texas Instruments, Analog Applications Journal 2Q 2013, pp. 29–30: https://www.ti.com/lit/an/slyt511/slyt511.pdf
  • Both resistive and capacitive technology predate the iPhone. Resistive screens can accept fingers; capacitive sensing alone does not imply multi-touch. Input hardware, controller, software, task, and environment jointly determine the interaction.
