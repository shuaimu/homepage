# Liquid Glass launch-era screenshot candidates

Selected assets now appear in the lecture: notifications-beto.jpg, control-holly.jpg, verge-beta1.png, and verge-beta2.png. The other assets remain research candidates. These concern the original iOS 26 developer beta in June 2025, not iOS 27. Image pixels remain unchanged. The two Verge downloads returned JPEG data despite their .png URLs; they were losslessly re-encoded as PNG for Typst compatibility. Complaints are individual reactions, not measured usability findings.

## notifications-beto.jpg

Beto Moedano, June 9, 2025: “I can’t see anything 😅”. White notification text over a pale blue/green wallpaper. The post is contemporaneous with beta 1; the screenshot does not show its build number.

- Original post: https://twitter.com/betomoedano/status/1932189570604437886
- Original image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GtCELInWkAIjd6a.jpg

## control-holly.jpg

Holly (@AnxiousHolly), June 10, 2025: “yeah i cant defend this”. Control Center over Home Screen icons/widgets, shown inside a device frame. The post is contemporaneous with beta 1; the image does not display its build number.

- Original post: https://twitter.com/AnxiousHolly/status/1932330130967257360
- Original image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GtEEAkxWQAEo9MV.jpg

## notifications-stammy.jpg

Paul Stamatiou (@Stammy), June 9, 2025: “this just seems harder to read? #ios26”. An image of Apple's demonstration with notification text over dog/grass wallpaper. This is a reaction to the presentation, not Stamatiou's own installed-beta screenshot.

- Original post: https://twitter.com/Stammy/status/1932124696562061655
- Original image: https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GtBI7whWQAAOSWS.jpg

Post text and image URLs were retrieved from X's public tweet embed endpoint, cdn.syndication.twimg.com/tweet-result. These posts are also embedded in contemporaneous coverage: https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250624-apple-liquid-glass-control-center

## verge-beta1.png and verge-beta2.png
